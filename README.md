## 🔍 Detailed Topic Breakdown

### 1. 🌊 Boundary Layer in Fluid Dynamics
*Focus: Viscous flow behavior, asymptotic approximations, and aerodynamics.*
- **Historical Background:** Ludwig Prandtl's boundary layer hypothesis (1904) and high Reynolds number flow regimes.
- **Navier-Stokes Simplification:** Order-of-magnitude analysis reducing full Navier-Stokes equations to Prandtl's boundary layer equations.
- **Flow Characteristics:** Laminar vs. turbulent boundary layer growth, velocity profiles, and wall shear stress.
- **Characteristic Thicknesses:** Nominal thickness ($\delta$), displacement thickness ($\delta^*$), momentum thickness ($	heta$), and energy thickness.
- **Flow Separation & Aerodynamics:** Adverse pressure gradients, boundary layer separation, wake formation, and drag reduction principles.

### 2. 🔬 Complex Analysis
*Focus: Holomorphic functions, contour integration, and residue calculus.*
- **Analytic Functions:** Complex differentiability, Cauchy-Riemann equations in Cartesian and polar coordinates, harmonic conjugates.
- **Complex Integration:** Line integrals along smooth curves, Jordan Curve Theorem, and Cauchy-Goursat Theorem.
- **Integral Formulas:** Cauchy's Integral Formula and Cauchy's differentiation formula for higher-order derivatives.
- **Series Expansions:** Taylor series representation of analytic functions and Laurent series expansions in annular domains.
- **Singularities & Residues:** Classification of isolated singularities (removable, poles, essential) and evaluating real definite integrals using the Residue Theorem.

### 3. ⚙️ Mathematical Methods
*Focus: Analytical techniques for solving mathematical physics boundary value problems.*
- **Integral Transforms:**
  - Laplace Transforms: Properties, convolution theorem, Dirac delta, and solving linear initial-value ODE systems.
  - Fourier Transforms: Fourier integral representation, sine and cosine transforms, frequency domain filtering.
- **Special Functions:** Gamma and Beta functions, Bessel functions ($J_n, Y_n$), Legendre polynomials ($P_n(x)$), and generating functions.
- **Sturm-Liouville Theory:** Self-adjoint differential operators, eigenvalue problems, and generalized orthogonal function expansions.
- **Green's Function Technique:** Constructing fundamental solutions for non-homogeneous boundary value problems.

### 4. 📈 Partial Differential Equations (PDEs)
*Focus: Second-order linear equations and classic boundary value formulations.*
- **Classification of Second-Order PDEs:**
  - **Elliptic:** Laplace and Poisson Equations (equilibrium, potential theory).
  - **Parabolic:** Heat and Diffusion Equation (time-dependent dissipation).
  - **Hyperbolic:** Wave Equation (propagation and vibrations).
- **Separation of Variables:** Complete derivation process for 1D and 2D homogeneous boundary value problems.
- **Fourier Series Application:** Matching orthogonal series solutions with initial and boundary Dirichlet/Neumann conditions.
- **D'Alembert's Solution:** Characteristic coordinates and traveling wave solutions for the 1D wave equation.
